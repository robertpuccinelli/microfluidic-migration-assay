## Microfluidic Migration Assay

### Overview

Sophia Levan of the DeRisi lab at UCSF requested microfluidic devices to study migration of macrophages obtained from primary isolates.

### Changelog

#### Rev A - 20250110
Initial device design based upon the design presented by Pérez-Rodríguez in [https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-022-02623-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-022-02623-w)
